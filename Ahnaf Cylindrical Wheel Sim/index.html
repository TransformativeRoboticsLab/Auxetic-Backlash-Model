Captures alpha, backlash, ring/row counts, axial pitch, attachment sites, and every cell's role/alpha.

Each ring closes exactly by construction when every cell shares one alpha (n single-pin joints, turn angle 2*pi/n per joint). Actuating or locking individual cells makes per-cell alpha genuinely vary, so the same fixed-turn construction generally will not land back on its own start - the "ring closure residual" then measures how inconsistent the per-cell commands are with a physically closed ring, which the backlash-gated coupling above is what keeps small in practice. Wheel diameter is derived from cell pitch and count, not chosen directly, and can now differ row to row (see the diameter range above) since each row is its own ring solved from that row's own per-cell alphas. Drive is the paper's alpha dilation factor with the reported theta = 70*alpha - 60 angle law. Material Restriction checks clearance between circumferential and axial neighbor cells only (not the full n*m x n*m pair set). This still reuses the row primitive's single "A-B" pin relation rather than its mirrored double-pin closure, and axial row-to-row attachment is a rigid z-offset copy rather than a solved joint - both remain natural next steps before this is a fabrication-level wheel model.
